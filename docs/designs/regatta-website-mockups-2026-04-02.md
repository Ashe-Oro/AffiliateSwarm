## Mockup 1 - Harbor Ledger

Intent:

- Trust-first, calm maritime visual language.
- Emphasize escrow and verification confidence.
- Make the agent + human operating model explicit in hero language.

What changed:

- Soft atmospheric gradients and card depth replacing flat monochrome blocks.
- Balanced role split CTA (advertiser + affiliate).
- Compact operator panel introducing proof signals early.

Best fit if:

- You want a premium but approachable homepage that still feels operational.

## Mockup 2 - Editorial Signal

Intent:

- Story-driven homepage with strong narrative rhythm.
- Reframe Regatta as category-defining, not only technical tooling.
- Put "built for agents and humans" at the center of the narrative frame.

What changed:

- Editorial serif headline system with warmer palette.
- Message architecture panel to show cleaner copy strategy directly in the design.
- Comparative framing blocks (Current / Alternative / Impact).

Best fit if:

- You want stronger brand voice and memorability for top-of-funnel audiences.

## Mockup 3 - Agent Ops

Intent:

- Technical confidence and systems-level clarity.
- Show Regatta as an operational control layer for agent-led growth.
- Clarify shared responsibility between autonomous agents and human operators.

What changed:

- Dark command-center visual style with animated grid and staggered signal cards.
- High-density signal panel (verification pass, escrow volume, payout lag).
- Suggested homepage structure embedded in the mockup itself.

Best fit if:

- You want a product-forward and performance-oriented brand direction.

## Suggested Next Step

Pick one direction as the base and then:

1. Merge that visual system into `src/app/page.tsx`.
2. Apply the copy simplification plan from `docs/designs/website-copy-review-2026-04-02.md`.
3. Keep one canonical "Tell your agent" pattern and one fee model statement across pages.
